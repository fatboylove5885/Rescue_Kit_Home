A few useful answers

What can I make when I have almost no energy?

Choose minimal prep and about 10 minutes. A rotisserie chicken plate, hummus wraps or a microwave bean bowl are practical starting points. Use ready-to-eat ingredients and follow the full recipe.

Does the ingredient filter mean I have everything I need?

No. It helps you choose a starting ingredient. Open the recipe to check the complete shopping list, quantities, labels and cooking directions before you begin.

Can I save the same filters for another day?

Yes. Bookmark the page after choosing your filters, or use “Share this dinner finder.” The link remembers those selections. You can reset them any time.

Are the pictures exact recipe photographs?

The cards use illustrative imagery from our guides. Some visuals are AI-created serving suggestions or original drawings. Follow the written recipe for ingredients and portions; the finder is not a claim that every dish has been test-cooked.
