Privacy Information

Updated October 18, 2026 — draft for site-owner review before publication. This page explains how What's for Eats uses site analytics, optional printable requests and links to other services. The site owner must add verified contact information and confirm the actual cookie and retention settings before this draft is published.

Information you choose to submit

When you request a free printable, the form may collect your email address, requested resource, source page and whether you separately opted in to receive marketing emails. This information is used to deliver the requested resource, manage the request and, only if you opted in, send occasional dinner-planning emails. A printable request does not require you to consent to marketing. Do not enter sensitive personal information.

How email delivery works

Some forms use a Google Apps Script service and a Google Sheet configured by the site operator for request tracking and delivery. Delivery may use Google email services. Delivery partners process the information needed for that service. The site operator should confirm storage, access, retention and security settings before publishing this notice.

Website analytics and cookies

Parts of the site load Google's analytics tools to help understand visits and page interactions. Depending on the tools' configuration, these services may use cookies or similar browser technologies and may receive device information, browser details, approximate location, and interaction data. For more information about Google's practices, consult Google's Privacy Policy. The site operator must verify whether a cookie consent mechanism is required for its visitors and configure it appropriately.

Optional paid products and external sites

Links to paid dinner-planning products or third-party websites may take you away from What's for Eats. Those sites have their own privacy and purchase policies. Review them before submitting payment details. This site does not ask visitors to submit payment-card information through its blog content.

Future display advertising

If the site begins showing advertising through Google AdSense or another vendor, this notice and any legally required consent experience must be updated before ads launch to identify advertising vendors, cookies, personalization options and applicable opt-outs. Google's published publisher requirements explain that Google and third-party ad vendors may use cookies to display ads based on prior visits. Visitors can review Google's ad personalization settings. This statement describes a potential future use and does not by itself mean that advertising is active.

Your choices and contact

You can choose not to request a printable, and you can leave the marketing opt-in unchecked. If you subscribed to marketing emails, use the unsubscribe option included in those messages. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with an email request, contact the site operator through the verified contact method shown on the website. Site-owner action required: publish an actual monitored contact email address or contact form before deploying this policy.

Privacy obligations differ by location. This general notice is not a substitute for a review of applicable laws and the services actually configured on the site.
