How do points and new boards work?

Each challenge earns 20 points once per board, each new bingo line earns 100, and a full board earns a 300-point bonus. The free center does not earn points. Undoing a mark updates your board without taking away points already earned; marking it again does not earn them twice. New boards keep your lifetime points and badges.

Can I play without cooking every day?

Absolutely. There is no deadline, daily streak or obligation to finish. Many squares are planning, assembly or cleanup wins. One completed action can count toward more than one relevant square. Choose what fits your household.

Will the PDF look like the game?

The download creates a crisp vector PDF with matching colors, square order, challenge titles and optional completion marks. Screen animations, emoji artwork and clickable game controls stay in HTML; the PDF uses drawn decorations and an easy-to-print layout. Turn off “Include my marked squares” for a fresh paper board.

Where does my progress live?

Boards, checked squares, points and badges save in this browser on this device. They do not sync between devices. Clearing browser data removes them. Downloads provide a paper snapshot, not a game backup.
